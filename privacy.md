# SwitchTrader Backup — Privacy Policy

SwitchTrader Backup is a personal, single-user utility that copies its own configuration and history files to its owner's Google Drive account.

It collects no data from anyone, stores no third-party information, uses no cookies or analytics, and shares nothing with any other party. The only Google account it accesses is the owner's own, with the owner's explicit authorization, solely to store backup files the tool itself created.
